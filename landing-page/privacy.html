Privacy

What this site and the extension touch.

Short, because the waitlist is the only thing we store. The product itself does not phone home.

Waitlist

If you submit the form, we store your email so we can tell you when a packaged install exists. We do not use it for a newsletter. You can ask to be removed.

The extension

The extension reads YouTube video identifiers and metadata so it can queue videos. If you process a video through Gemini Web, it reads conversation content in that tab in order to import the note.

Transcripts and notes

Transcripts and notes go to your native helper, your Cloudflare worker, and Gemini. Not to Cool Cool Cool. Not to this website.

What we do not do

No product analytics. No sale of data. No third-party ads.

This website

The landing page is static. Fonts load from Google Fonts. The waitlist form posts to our waitlist endpoint.

Contact

For waitlist removal or questions, open an issue on the GitHub repository.
