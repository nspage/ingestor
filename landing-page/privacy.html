Privacy

What this site and the extension touch.

The product itself does not phone home. You run your own worker and your own model key.

This website

The landing page is static. Fonts load from Google Fonts. We do not collect an email here.

The extension

The extension reads YouTube video identifiers and metadata so it can queue videos. If you process a video through Gemini Web, it reads conversation content in that tab in order to import the note.

Transcripts and notes

Transcripts and notes go to your native helper, your Cloudflare worker, and your OpenRouter or Gemini account. Not to Cool Cool Cool. Not to this website.

What we do not do

No product analytics. No sale of data. No third-party ads. No shared model key.

Questions: open an issue on the GitHub repository.
